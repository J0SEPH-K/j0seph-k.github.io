# Awwwards references and design decisions

Researched 2026-10-19. These references informed principles and interaction choices; the portfolio pages are original compositions and do not reproduce the source designs.

## References

- [Escape Coffee — About page element](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/about-us-page-escape-coffee-escape-coffee): Awwwards lists a full-screen image, text animation, scroll, and parallax. Applied to the restaurant's image-led opening and long editorial transition into the menu.
- [Escape Coffee — coffee nominees](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/coffee/): category context for visual storytelling in food and drink.
- [Gelato La Boca — restaurant nominees](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/restaurant/?page=3): listed as Site of the Day and Developer Award (Nov 2, 2024). Used as a reference for treating a small food brand as a distinct, memorable experience rather than a generic menu template.
- [Serenity Hair Salon Blaxland — Awwwards nominee listing](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/web-interactive/?page=5): used as a salon-specific reference point for expressive identity tied to a local service.
- [Kōa Surf Academy — Awwwards nominee listing](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/%230E100F/): used as a reference for making an education/sports brand feel active and place-specific.
- [Awwwards interactive websites](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/web-interactive/?page=5): category guidance around interaction as part of the user journey, not decoration by itself.
- [Awwwards dynamic grid examples](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/dynamic-grid-layout-examples/): informed the salon's horizontal, variable-width work gallery and the index page's asymmetric composition.

## What changed in this iteration

- Each category now has a different composition and customer journey: restaurant menu discovery, wellness orientation, salon portfolio browsing, florist product building, fitness program selection, academy interest matching, skincare consultation, and home-service estimation.
- Scroll drives reveal transitions and a top-of-page progress indicator. Every page has enough distinct sections to tell a complete visit story.
- Small interactions directly support the business task: filter menu items, change class schedules, drag/swipe a salon gallery, build a bouquet, run a breathing timer, match a class, choose consultation concerns, and compare work photos/calculate an estimate.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; mobile layouts collapse to a single column and keep the key actions available.

## Evidence and limits

Awwwards listings show awards, categories, and some showcased elements. They do not establish that any one visual treatment will increase conversion for a Korean neighborhood business. These are creative directions to help owners choose a brand feel; confirm the business's real audience, services, assets, and operational needs before making a production site.
