The root page is a visual index. Each business demo has its own layout, type system, color story, scroll sequence, and interaction. The pages are static HTML/CSS/JS and do not need a build step.

## Concepts

1. The Table — restaurant, menu-led cinematic story
2. Slow Form — pilates and wellness, calm guided visit
3. Forme — salon, fashion editorial and swipeable work gallery
4. Petal Press — florist, playful seasonal catalogue and bouquet builder
5. Base 24 — fitness, kinetic training story and breathing timer
6. Moa Class — academy, colorful project story and interest matcher
7. Serein — skincare, quiet service narrative and consultation selector
8. Field Notes — home services, before/after comparison and estimate calculator

Names, photos, prices, hours, testimonials, and addresses are illustrative concept material. Replace with verified client information before using a page as a real business website.
